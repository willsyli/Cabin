Product: Agentic Engine Optimization (AEO)
Objective: Create the first analytics and optimization platform that helps travel brands (Expedia, Booking.com, TripAdvisor, etc.) measure and improve how their MCP endpoints perform in agent-driven ecosystems.

1) Market Need
- Teams are shipping MCP endpoints but cannot see how often or why agents choose them.
- No optimization feedback loops; MCPs exist in isolation with no ranking or reputation layer.

2) Target Users
- Enterprise API/MCP engineering teams, AI partnership leads, product/data analysts in the travel vertical.

3) Competitive Landscape
- GEO prompt-level tools: Bear AI, Evertune, Profound (optimize output text), not protocol-level AEO.
- API observability tools measure usage, not agent selection/visibility.
- Anthropic MCP SDKs provide infra, not analytics or optimization.

4) Differentiator
- First scoring model and telemetry hooks for MCP visibility and trust.
- Benchmarking layer to compare Expedia vs Booking vs TripAdvisor performance on standardized intents.

5) MVP & Roadmap
- Phase 1: Visibility Dashboard (agent simulations + scoring).
- Phase 2: AEO SDK (auto instrumentation + /aeo/* endpoints).

6) Opportunity
- As protocols converge (MCP + closed Skills), AEO becomes the “Search Console for agents.”
